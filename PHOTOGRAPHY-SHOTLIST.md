# Dumas Group — Photography Shot List

The concept build uses licensed stock photography as placeholders. These twelve shots, captured at Dumas Group's own operations, would replace them for the production site.

1. Coal stockpile with bucket-wheel excavator or dragline in operation — Mpumalanga (hero)
2. Wash plant structure, wide exterior shot showing scale
3. Loaded coal wagons on the rail siding
4. Conveyor detail — coal moving from stockpile to load-out
5. Chrome ore stockpile or concentrate close-up
6. Chrome processing plant exterior
7. Campbell Limestone Project quarry benches, Northern Cape
8. Limestone crusher or processing structure
9. Port gantry or ship loader during a load-out (if access permits)
10. Aerial of an open-cast pit showing bench structure and scale
11. AET Group solar or off-grid installation, Namibia
12. Boffin property development, exterior or site progress

## Notes for the shoot

- No people in frame — matches the concept build's design direction and avoids needing model releases before the site goes live.
- Aim for slightly overcast or early/late light — the concept build desaturates and tints all imagery toward the site's black, silver and graphite palette, so flat, even light will grade better than harsh midday sun.
- Wide shots that show scale (equipment against stockpile, trucks against a pit face) read better than close-up product shots for a due-diligence audience.
- Landscape orientation, highest resolution available. RAW or high-quality JPEG.
